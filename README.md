## Project Overview

This interactive Power BI dashboard provides an executive-level view of life insurance sales performance. It enables leadership to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), evaluate sales trends, analyze conversion metrics, and identify opportunities to improve business performance.

---

## Business Objective

Develop an executive dashboard that consolidates sales performance into a single reporting solution, allowing decision-makers to quickly assess production, conversion rates, referrals, and premium growth.

---

## Dashboard Features

- Executive KPI Cards
- Monthly ALP Trend Analysis
- Sales vs. Sits Comparison
- Sales Conversion Funnel
- Lead Source Performance
- Monthly Close Ratio
- Referral Trend Analysis
- Product Mix Analysis
- ALP Goal Tracking Gauge
- Monthly Summary Table

---

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Annual Premium (ALP)
- Total Sales
- Total Sits
- Appointments Booked
- Close Ratio
- Show Ratio
- Total Referrals Collected

## Key Insights

- Annual Premium (ALP) totaled **$417,692** during the reporting period.
- The overall **Close Ratio** was **66.7%**.
- The overall **Show Ratio** was **69.1%**.
- Premium production peaked in **September**, while lower production was observed during the final quarter.
- Referral performance and lead source comparisons provide visibility into the highest-performing acquisition channels.
